### About me

I'm an Artificial Intelligence student based in Jeddah, Saudi Arabia. I work where **AI, design, and hardware** meet: I build models that solve real problems, design the interfaces that make them usable, and understand the physical side of a product.

- 🧠 **Machine Learning:** co-authored research on early detection of epileptic seizures from EEG signals using XGBoost
- 🎨 **Design:** led design teams at the AI Club, GDG on Campus UJ, and the Elm Club
- 🛠️ **Maker:** I build and fly FPV drones and work with 3D printing and 3D modeling (Fusion 360)
- 🎯 **Looking for:** a co-op training opportunity in AI/ML engineering or AI product, leading to a full-time role
